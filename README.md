# 🧭 Rehala | رحالة ✈️

## 📌 Project Overview

**Rehala** (رحالة) is an innovative smart tourism platform and mobile application designed to connect travelers with certified local guides and fellow travelers, making exploring new destinations easier, more interactive, and more authentic.

The app organizes travel planning by matching users with similar destination interests, providing personalized itineraries, and offering interactive guides for hidden gems and local experiences.

It also includes an **AI-powered feature** that allows users to scan locations, receive instant recommendations, and discover historical and cultural insights about the places they visit.

The project aims to make travel planning seamless, affordable, and deeply connected to local cultures through a simple, engaging, and interactive digital experience.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Make traveling and trip planning **fun, smart, and accessible**.
* Connect solo travelers with travel companions and local guides.
* Organize travel journeys into clear, customized itineraries and smart schedules.
* Add an interactive review and rating system to help users share real experiences.
* Use AI to scan tourist locations and provide instant insights and recommendations.
* Make local and international tourism easier to explore for **different types of travelers**.

---

## 📦 Project Scope

### In Scope
* Tourist destinations and local attractions as the initial content focus.
* Companion matching & local guide booking structure.
* AI-powered location scanner and smart recommendations.
* Customized trip planning and itinerary generator.
* Interactive community for reviews and sharing experiences.
* Arabic-first (and multilingual) user experience.
